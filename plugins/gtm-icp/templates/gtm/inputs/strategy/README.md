# inputs/strategy/

Where the business is going. The module reads this folder for direction only: who you want to sell to next. It never treats it as evidence of who buys today.

## What goes here

- `intake-answers.md`: your answers to the module's opening questions. The module writes it for you, so next time it doesn't ask again.
- Strategy documents: plans for a new market, product, price or segment.
- Existing personas, segment files or journey maps. Useful to compare with what the data shows.
- Internal positioning or messaging documents.

When the data and the strategy point at different customers, the module shows both side by side and asks you which one to build on. It never picks for you.

This folder stays on your machine and out of git.

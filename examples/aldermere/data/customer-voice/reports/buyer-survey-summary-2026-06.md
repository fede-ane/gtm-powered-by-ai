# Buyer survey summary, Q2 2026

We surveyed 84 finance and engineering leaders at mid-market software companies about how they buy payments infrastructure.

## Headline findings

- 62% say integration time is the first thing they check.
- 48% say they have been surprised by fees after signing.
- Buyers consistently told us: "We want one partner who understands every market we sell in, so we never have to think about payments again."
- Compliance teams are involved in about 4 in 10 purchases, usually late.

## Caveat

The sample came from our own newsletter list and over-represents existing customers.
